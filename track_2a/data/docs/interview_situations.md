# Application situations and preparation

Sources: *Unterschiedliche Bewerbungssituationen* and *Allgemeine Vorbereitungen auf ein Vorstellungsgespräch* (FHGR / Profolio). The texts are summarised and translated here; the German originals are not included in this repository. The same situations appear as values of `situation` in the questions, scenarios and transcripts.

## Situations

| `situation` | German term | Purpose | What matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| `phone_call_trial` | Anruf in einem Betrieb (Bewerbung um eine Schnupperlehre) | Show interest, introduce yourself briefly, arrange a date for a trial apprenticeship | Friendly, polite tone; speak clearly; give your **full name**. Content: greeting and introduction (name, school, class), reason for the call, ask for a Schnuppertag, accept or propose a date, say thank you and goodbye. Preparation: write down keywords and choose a quiet place. |
| *(info talk)* | Informations- oder Kennenlerngespräch | Get to know an occupation or company before applying; ask questions | Often part of career exploration or a Schnupperlehre. Not modelled separately in the dataset. |
| `trial_interview` | Gespräch für eine Schnupperlehre / einen Schnuppertag | Often the **first formal conversation** | Focus on motivation, interest in the occupation, reliability and expectations. Less formal than an apprenticeship interview. |
| `online_interview` | Online-Bewerbungsgespräch | Interview by video (e.g. Teams, Zoom) | Also needs media skills: technology, camera and sound, background, looking into the camera. |
| `apprenticeship_interview` | Vorstellungsgespräch für eine Lehrstelle | The classic interview; the company selects a suitable apprentice | Topics: interests and hobbies, motivation for the occupation and company, knowledge of both, strengths and weaknesses, school performance, social skills, career goals, the candidate's own questions. |
| `assessment` | Strukturiertes Auswahlgespräch (Assessment) | Mainly used by larger companies | Interview plus role plays, group tasks, case studies and short presentations. Social and personal skills are assessed as well as interest in the occupation. |
| `follow_up_interview` | Nachgespräch oder Zweitgespräch | After a Schnupperlehre or a first interview | Exchange impressions, clarify open questions, decide on next steps. |

## Preparation checklist (what a well-prepared candidate does)

The coach can use this list for pre-interview tips, and to probe the `preparation` criterion (e.g. «Wie hast du dich vorbereitet?»).

### In-person interview
- Find out about the company: locations, products or services, values (company website).
- Be on time, and plan for a missed train.
- The interview is **not** an information session about the occupation. The candidate should already know the occupation.
- Bring the application (printed, in a folder), plus a notepad and pen with **prepared questions** about the apprenticeship.
- No chewing gum, no cap or headphones; phone on silent and in the bag.

### Online interview
- Practise beforehand with a trusted person: camera angle, sound, lighting, background, and get feedback on how you come across (eye contact, body language).
- Test the technology in time (camera, microphone, speakers, internet); have the link ready; log in 5–10 minutes early.
- Choose a quiet place with a tidy background. Turn off notifications. Dress appropriately. Have documents and prepared questions at hand.
- During the call: look into the camera, speak clearly, let the other person finish, show interest, and thank them at the end.

### Assessment
- Find out about the company and the apprenticeship. Think about why you want this occupation and this company. Reflect on your strengths, weaknesses and experiences. Practise a short self-introduction.
- Listen carefully to tasks and ask if something is unclear. Contribute ideas and respect others. Take responsibility in group tasks. Stay calm when a task is difficult.
- **Important:** the result is not the only thing assessed. *How* you approach the task, communicate and cooperate often matters just as much.

### Tips from Profolio on answering (from the Kriterienraster)
- Don't answer with one word. «Ja/Nein» is not enough.
- Give answers in a few meaningful sentences rather than rambling.
- You may think for a few seconds before answering.
- If you are unsure or didn't understand the question, you may ask.
- Don't speak badly of others. «Alle Lehrpersonen sind einfach doof» is not a good reason.
- Don't look at the floor or your hands; try to keep eye contact, as with friends.
